## Implementation

The source certificate is represented by a proposition indexed by remaining
coordinates, level, and realized function. Its zero constructor certifies only
the zero function. Its successor partitions the current coordinate register,
applies a supported linear map depending only on the head restriction, and
selects the recursively certified tail by an element of that head map's range.

Level inclusion prepends an empty zero head and retains the full original
certificate in the tail. This is an exact Lean alternative to the paper's
valid zero-to-one witness followed by structural induction, not paper gap G20.
The binary direct sum embeds both coordinate registers, recursively combines
equal-level certificates, and raises the inputs to `max level1 level2` first.

Downsize applies `FieldData.coordinates` pointwise, then flattens
`Fin n -> Fin k -> ZMod 2` through `finProdFinEquiv` and
`GaloisField.equivZmodP`. Every selected source coordinate expands to its full
block of `k` binary coordinates. Each source head map is restricted to
`ZMod 2`, conjugated by the coordinate equivalence, and rebundled over
`GaloisField 2 1`.

The construction applies the tracked G21 correction: a target range prefix is
pulled back through `downsizeVector.symm` before selecting the source recursive
branch. The separate zero-certificate branch proves the source-permitted
`level = 0` case omitted by the paper proof. These are internal repairs and do
not change a frozen declaration.
